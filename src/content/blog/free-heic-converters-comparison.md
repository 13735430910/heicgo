## heicgo.com — Best for privacy + EXIF

**Pros:** Client-side conversion (no upload), EXIF preserved, batch conversion, quality slider, free.
**Cons:** New site, fewer advanced editing options.

## HEICfree — Decent all-rounder

**Pros:** Client-side, batch mode, ZIP download.
**Cons:** No EXIF preservation, no quality control beyond a slider.

## PicShift — Most polished UI

**Pros:** Beautiful interface, detailed quality options, Safari native acceleration.
**Cons:** No EXIF preservation, not open source.

## CloudConvert — Most features

**Pros:** 200+ format support, EXIF preserved, API access.
**Cons:** Files uploaded to cloud servers, 25 free conversions/day limit.

## The privacy question

The biggest dividing line is **upload vs client-side**. CloudConvert and similar services process files on their servers — they can see your photos. heicgo, HEICfree, and PicShift do everything in your browser.

## Recommendation

For most users: **[heicgo.com](/) — free, private, and preserves EXIF.**
