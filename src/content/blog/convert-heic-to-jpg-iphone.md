Converting HEIC to JPG on iPhone is simple — Apple provides built-in options, and there are free third-party tools.

## Method 1: Change iPhone Camera Settings

To stop taking HEIC photos entirely:

1. Settings → Camera → Formats
2. Select **"Most Compatible"** instead of "High Efficiency"
3. Future photos will be JPEG

This doesn't convert existing HEIC photos — it only affects new ones.

## Method 2: Email to Yourself

iOS automatically converts HEIC to JPEG when sharing:

1. Select photos in Photos app
2. Tap Share → Mail
3. Send to yourself
4. Download the JPEG attachments

Limitations: Limited to small batches (5-10 photos), reduces resolution, strips EXIF.

## Method 3: Files App Conversion

1. Open Photos → select photos → Share → Save to Files
2. Open Files app → find the saved HEIC files
3. Long press → Quick Actions → Convert Image
4. Choose JPEG format

This only works on iOS 16+ and converts one file at a time.

## Method 4: heicgo.com in Safari (Best)

1. Open Safari → go to [heicgo.com](/)
2. Tap "Browse files" → select HEIC photos
3. Adjust quality (92% recommended)
4. Tap "Convert to JPG"
5. Download individual files or "Download All (ZIP)"

This is the only method that:
- Preserves EXIF metadata (GPS, date, camera info)
- Supports batch conversion
- Works entirely on-device (no upload)
- Is completely free
