The average iPhone holds 3,000+ photos — most of them never looked at again. Here's how to take control.

## 1. Delete Duplicates First

iOS 16+ has a built-in duplicate detector:

- Photos → Albums → Utilities → Duplicates
- Review and merge duplicates
- Each merged pair recovers storage

## 2. Use Albums, Not Just Camera Roll

Create albums for:
- **Events** (Wedding 2024, Japan Trip)
- **People** (already auto-curated by Photos app)
- **Projects** (House renovation, eBay listings)
- **Documents** (screenshots of receipts, IDs)

## 3. Enable iCloud Photos with Optimization

Settings → Photos → Optimize iPhone Storage

Your 50 GB library might only use 2-3 GB on-device with full-resolution versions in iCloud.

## 4. Clean Up Screenshots

Search "Screenshot" or "Screen Recording" in Photos. Delete:
- Old memes and jokes
- One-time-use screenshots (directions, temporary info)
- Duplicate screenshots

Most users can safely delete 50-80% of their screenshots.

## 5. Convert HEIC Archives to JPEG

For photos you want to keep long-term but don't need in full quality:

1. Transfer HEIC files to computer
2. Batch convert at 80% quality using [heicgo.com](/)
3. Store the compressed JPEGs as your archive
4. Delete original HEICs from device

## 6. Use Search and Smart Recognition

The Photos app can search by:
- Objects: "dog", "car", "beach", "food"
- Places: "Tokyo", "home", "work"
- People: recognized faces
- Text in photos: "menu", "receipt"

## 7. Hide Sensitive Photos

Photos → Select → Share → Hide

Hidden photos go to a separate album (Albums → Hidden) that requires Face ID to view.

## 8. Batch Edit Multiple Photos

Select multiple photos → Edit one → Copy Edits → Paste Edits on others

Great for applying the same filter or crop to an entire batch.

## 9. Set Up Automated Backups

Option A: iCloud (automatic, paid after 5 GB)
Option B: Google Photos (15 GB free, auto-backup)
Option C: Both (3-2-1 backup rule)

## 10. Monthly Photo Review Habit

Spend 10 minutes each month:

1. Delete obviously bad photos (blurry, accidental shots)
2. Remove duplicates from the duplicates album
3. Archive converted JPEGs to external storage
4. Clear Recently Deleted (saves space immediately)

This habit prevents libraries from becoming unmanageable over years.
