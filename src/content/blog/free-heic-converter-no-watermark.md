## Why Watermarks Exist

Free converters add watermarks because:

1. Server costs — HEIC conversion on a server uses CPU/GPU resources
2. User acquisition — watermarks drive visitors back to their site
3. Paid tiers — watermark removal is a premium feature

## How heicgo.com Avoids Watermarks

Upload-based converters (even watermark-free ones) have another cost: your data. Your photos sit on their servers during conversion. Client-side converters like heicgo keep everything local.
